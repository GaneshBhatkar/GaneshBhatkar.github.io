Research Interests & Proposed Direction

Autonomous Mobile Robots and Unmanned Ground Vehicles

My research interests focus on developing reliable autonomous mobile robots by combining embedded systems, robot perception, state estimation, localization, mapping, navigation, and vehicle dynamics.

The topic presented on this page is a proposed research direction and should not be interpreted as completed academic research.

Primary Research Interest

I am interested in how the mechanical behavior of a mobile robot affects the reliability of its perception and localization systems, particularly when the robot operates on uneven terrain or in constrained environments.

Proposed Research Topic

Effect of Vehicle Tilting and Rear Suspension Dynamics on Visual-SLAM Accuracy in a Three-Wheeled Autonomous Ground Vehicle

The proposed direction is to study the relationship between vehicle motion, chassis tilting, suspension behavior, and SLAM performance in a three-wheeled autonomous ground vehicle.

The central motivation is that changes in vehicle attitude and vibration may influence sensor measurements, camera motion, feature tracking, and the quality of the estimated robot trajectory and map.

Potential Research Questions

Vehicle Dynamics

  • How does chassis tilting influence the robot's motion and sensor orientation?
  • How do rear suspension characteristics affect body vibration?
  • Which operating conditions produce the greatest attitude variation?

Perception and SLAM

  • How does vehicle motion affect visual feature tracking?
  • Does increased vibration reduce Visual-SLAM trajectory consistency?
  • How can SLAM accuracy be evaluated under different motion conditions?

Autonomous Navigation

  • How can localization reliability be improved on uneven terrain?
  • Can vehicle attitude information support more robust estimation?
  • What trade-offs exist between stability, mobility, and perception?

Possible Methodology

1. Vehicle and Environment Modeling

Develop a simulation model of a three-wheeled ground vehicle with configurable geometry, wheel arrangement, suspension behavior, and terrain conditions.

2. Motion and Sensor Simulation

Simulate vehicle tilting, body vibration, camera motion, IMU measurements, and other relevant sensor effects.

3. SLAM Evaluation

Compare trajectory error, map consistency, tracking quality, and localization stability under different vehicle-dynamics conditions.

4. Analysis and Improvement

Identify the conditions that degrade SLAM performance and investigate possible improvements through sensor placement, filtering, estimation, or vehicle-design changes.
